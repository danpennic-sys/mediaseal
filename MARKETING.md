# MediaSeal Marketing Notes

## Positioning

**MediaSeal** — cryptographic proof that the photos and videos you took on site are exactly the files you claim.

Field techs lose jobs and get chargebacks when clients say “that photo is old” or “the video was edited.” MediaSeal turns a folder of phone photos into a single, client-verifiable QuantumGuard evidence pack. The client (or their lawyer) can check the hashes with the free public verifier. No account. No cloud. No subscription.

Fits the Operator Pages suite:
- Capture with FieldSeal / JobSeal
- Seal media with MediaSeal
- Aggregate with LifecycleSeal
- Invoice and chase with Chaser

## Claims discipline

- Hashes the exact bytes of the media files.
- Does not claim EXIF authenticity (EXIF can be stripped or forged; we record what the filesystem sees).
- Does not embed the media itself in the pack (keeps packs small and offline-friendly).
- Verification is the free QuantumGuard CLI/HTTP.
